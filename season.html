Shot'o'Scotch

This spring semester we will present the section annual Shot'o'Scotch project! These are two productions that are smaller and lower committment than our typical mainstage shows.

These are low-tech and non-memorized read/sing throughs of one play and one musical -- we have not yet selected shows, so reach out to our Artistic Director if you have a specific show you'd like to see performed!
